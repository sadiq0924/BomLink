Free Fire sensitivity kaise set kare: har phone ke liye aasaan tareeka

By BomLink Team · · 6 min read

Har din koi na koi video bolta hai "ye sensitivity lagao, 100% headshot". Sach ye hai ki ek phone ki setting doosre phone par kaam nahi karti. Screen ka size, touch sampling aur aapki ungli ki speed, teeno alag hote hain. Is post me hum ek aisa tareeka dekhenge jisse aap 20 minute me apne phone ki sahi sensitivity khud nikaal sakte ho.

Sensitivity hoti kya hai?

Sensitivity batati hai ki screen par ungli thodi si chalane par camera kitna ghumega. Zyada sensitivity matlab chhoti si movement me bada turn. Kam sensitivity matlab aim control me rehta hai, lekin peeche mudne me time lagta hai.

Free Fire me 5 main options hote hain: - General: bina scope ke camera ghumana - Red Dot: red dot ya bina zoom wale scope ke saath - 2x Scope aur 4x Scope: zoom wali guns ke liye - Sniper Scope: AWM jaisi sniper ke liye

Step 1: Ek base se shuru karo

Sab options ko beech me rakho, jaise 50 ke aas paas. Ab Training Ground me jao. Wahan ke dummy targets par aim karna sabse sahi test hai, kyunki wahan koi pressure nahi hota.

Step 2: General sensitivity ka test

Ek target ke saamne khade ho jao aur ungli se ek baar mein 180 degree peeche mudo. Agar ek swipe me peeche nahi mud pa rahe, to General badhao. Agar camera zyada ghoom jaata hai aur target cross ho jaata hai, to thoda ghatao. Har baar 5 ka farak karo, ek saath bahut zyada mat badlo.

Step 3: Red Dot aur drag shot

Red Dot ko aise set karo ki fire dabate hi halka upar drag karne par crosshair body se head tak smooth jaaye, lekin head ke upar na nikle. Agar bar bar head ke upar ja raha hai, Red Dot ghatao. Agar chest par hi atak jaata hai, badhao.

Step 4: Scopes ko last me set karo

2x aur 4x scope me recoil zyada mehsoos hota hai. Inhe Red Dot se thoda kam rakhna aam taur par aasaan lagta hai. Sniper scope ko sabse kam rakho, taaki zoom karke chhoti si movement se target par tik sako.

Sabse zaroori baat: setting badalne ke baad kam se kam 3-4 din usi par khelo. Haath ko aadat padne me time lagta hai. Roz setting badalne se aim kabhi set nahi hota.

Galtiyan jo log karte hain

  • YouTube se kisi aur ke phone ki setting copy karna
  • Har haar ke baad setting badal dena
  • Sirf sensitivity ko dosh dena, practice na karna
  • Phone garam hone par test karna, jab touch slow ho jaata hai

Practice routine

Roz match se pehle 10 minute Training Ground: 3 minute general turn, 4 minute Red Dot drag shot, 3 minute moving target. Ek hafte me fark khud dikhne lagega.

Yaad rakho, sensitivity sirf aadha kaam karti hai. Baaki kaam crosshair placement aur shant dimaag karta hai.
