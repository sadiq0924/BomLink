Mobile se video editing seekh kar paise kaise kamaye: beginner guide

By BomLink Team · · 6 min read

Aaj har creator ko editor chahiye. Shorts, Reels aur gaming videos roz ban rahi hain, aur bahut se creators editing ka kaam bahar dete hain. Achhi baat ye hai ki shuruat ke liye laptop zaroori nahi, mobile se bhi seekh sakte ho.

Kaunsi app se shuru kare?

  • CapCut: beginners ke liye sabse aasaan, templates aur auto captions ke saath
  • VN Editor: timeline control achha hai, watermark nahi
  • Alight Motion: motion graphics aur smooth transitions ke liye

Pehle ek hi app pakdo aur use poori tarah seekho.

30 din ka seekhne ka plan

  • Hafta 1: cut, trim, speed, music sync aur basic transitions
  • Hafta 2: captions, text animation aur sound effects
  • Hafta 3: color grading, zoom effects aur hook banana
  • Hafta 4: 10 sample videos banao, alag alag style me

Roz ek chhota video edit karo, chahe kisi ka bhi clip lekar practice karo. Lekin practice wali videos ko apne naam se publish mat karna jab tak original creator se ijazat na ho.

Portfolio banao

Client ko aapke certificate se zyada aapka kaam dekhna hota hai. 5-8 best videos ka ek Google Drive folder ya Instagram page banao. Har video me before aur after dikhao, isse farak turant samajh aata hai.

Pehle clients kahan mile?

  • Chhote YouTubers aur Instagram creators ko seedha message karo, ek free sample edit offer karke
  • Fiverr jaise freelancing platform par profile banao
  • Local dukaan, coaching ya gym walon ke liye promo reel banao

Kitna charge kare?

Shuru me rate kam rakho taaki reviews aur experience mile. Kaam achha hone par dheere dheere rate badhao. Rate per video ya per minute dono tarah tay kar sakte ho, bas pehle se clear likh kar tay karo.

Galtiyan jo avoid karni hain

  • Bina advance ya platform ke bade kaam ka poora payment baad me lena
  • Copyright wala music bina soche use karna
  • Deadline miss karna. Ek late delivery client kho deti hai

Video editing aisi skill hai jiski demand aage bhi badhegi. Shuru ke 2-3 mahine seekhne me lagao, phir kamai apne aap shuru hoti hai.
