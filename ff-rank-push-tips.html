Free Fire rank push: solo aur squad dono ke liye 8 kaam ke tips

By BomLink Team · · 6 min read

Rank push me sabse badi galti hai har match me sirf kills ke peeche bhaagna. Rank points me survival aur placement ka bhi bada hissa hota hai. Agar aap jaldi mar jaate ho, to 5 kill bhi zyada kaam nahi aate. Chaliye dekhte hain kaise samajhdaari se rank badhaya jaata hai.

1. Drop location soch samajh kar chuno

Hot drop matlab jahan sab utarte hain, wahan jaldi marne ka chance zyada hai. Rank push ke time map ke kinare wali jagah chuno jahan loot theek ho aur log kam aate hon. Pehle 3-4 minute shaanti se loot karna baad me bahut kaam aata hai.

2. Loot me time waste mat karo

Pehle armor, helmet aur ek achhi primary gun. Phir medkit aur gloo wall. Har ghar ka har kona dekhne ki zaroorat nahi. Zyada der ek jagah rukoge to zone aur enemy dono pakad lenge.

3. Zone ke saath chalo, zone ke peeche nahi

Zone ke edge se andar aana safe hota hai, kyunki ek side se khatra kam ho jaata hai. Beech me rukne se chaaron taraf se fire aa sakta hai. Agla zone dikhte hi pehle se position bana lo.

4. Gloo wall ko habit banao

Jaise hi fire aaye, pehla kaam gloo wall. Healing hamesha cover ke peeche. Khule me medkit lagana sabse aam galti hai.

5. Height ka fayda uthao

Upar se neeche dekhne wale player ko zyada dikhta hai aur wo kam dikhta hai. Pahadi, chhat ya upar wali jagah mile to use mat chhodo.

6. Squad me role fix karo

  • Rusher: aage jaata hai, close fight leta hai
  • Support: gloo wall, revive aur healing sambhalta hai
  • Sniper / Scout: door se dekhta hai aur info deta hai
  • IGL: decide karta hai kahan jaana hai

Jab sab ek hi kaam karte hain, to team bikhar jaati hai.

7. Voice ya quick chat use karo

"Enemy left me", "gloo laga raha hoon" jaisi chhoti info bhi match jita deti hai. Bolna nahi chahte to quick chat buttons hi use karo.

8. Tilt hone par ruk jao

Lagataar 2-3 match haar gaye to break lo. Gusse me khelne se aur galtiyan hoti hain aur rank girta hai.

Ek simple rule: pehle 10 minute survive karo, aakhri 5 minute me fight lo. Top 5 me pahunchna rank ke liye kills se zyada keemti hota hai.

Rank ek din me nahi badhta. Roz 4-5 dhyan se khele gaye match, 15 bina soche match se zyada fayda dete hain.
