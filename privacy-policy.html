Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

BomLink ("hum") aapki privacy ka samman karta hai. Ye page batata hai ki is website par kaunsi jaankari li jaati hai aur uska kya use hota hai.

Hum kaunsi jaankari lete hain

  • Visit count: kaunsa page kitni baar khula, iska gina hua number. Isme aapka naam, email ya IP address save nahi hota.
  • Browser storage: aapke browser me chhoti si jaankari rakhi jaati hai, jaise aaj aap page dekh chuke hain ya nahi, aur cookie notice band kiya ya nahi.
  • Contact form: agar aap form bharte hain to aapka naam, email aur message hum tak pahunchta hai, sirf jawab dene ke liye.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase: visit count aur contact messages save karne ke liye. Google ki privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • News feed: "Latest news" section Google News aur RSS services (jaise rss2json) se khabrein laata hai. Ye services aapke browser ki request dekh sakti hain.
  • Google Fonts: website ke font ke liye.

Ads aur cookies

Is website par third-party ad networks ke ads dikh sakte hain. Ye ad networks aapko relevant ads dikhane ke liye cookies ya isi tarah ki technology use kar sakte hain. Agar Google ads dikhaye jaate hain, to Google DART cookie ka use kar sakta hai. Aap personalized ads band karne ke liye Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com) par ja sakte hain.

Aap apne browser ki settings se cookies block ya delete kar sakte hain. Aisa karne par kuch features theek se kaam nahi bhi kar sakte.

Bahar ke links

Hamari posts me doosri websites ke links ho sakte hain. Un websites ki privacy policy alag hoti hai aur unke content ki zimmedari hamari nahi hai.

Bachchon ki privacy

Ye website 13 saal se kam umar ke bachchon se jaan-boojh kar koi personal jaankari nahi leti.
