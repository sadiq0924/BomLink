UPI fraud se kaise bache: 7 tareeke jo har mobile user ko pata hone chahiye

By BomLink Team · · 5 min read

UPI ne payment aasaan bana diya, lekin isi ke saath naye tarah ke fraud bhi badhe hain. Zyaadatar fraud technology se nahi, balki jaldbaazi aur dar ka fayda uthakar hote hain. Ye 7 baatein yaad rakhoge to bahut had tak safe rahoge.

1. Paise lene ke liye PIN kabhi nahi lagta

Ye sabse zaroori rule hai. Agar koi kehta hai "paise bhejne ke liye request bheji hai, PIN daalo", to samjho fraud hai. UPI PIN sirf paise bhejne ke liye lagta hai.

2. QR code scan = paise jaana

QR code scan karke aap paise bhejte ho, lete nahi. Koi "inaam ke paise" ke liye QR scan karne ko kahe to mana kar do.

3. OTP aur PIN kisi ko mat batao

Bank, company ya customer care kabhi phone par OTP ya PIN nahi maangte. Chahe aawaz kitni bhi official lage.

4. Screen share apps se savdhan

Kuch fraud "help" ke naam par screen share ya remote control app install karwate hain. Iske baad wo aapka OTP aur sab kuch dekh sakte hain. Anjaan logon ke kehne par koi app install mat karo.

5. Customer care number Google se mat uthao

Fake numbers search results me bhi aa jaate hain. Hamesha company ki official app ya website se hi number lo.

6. "Galti se paise aa gaye, wapas bhejo"

Koi kehe ki galti se aapko paise bhej diye, to pehle apni bank app me balance khud check karo. Aksar message nakli hota hai aur paise aaye hi nahi hote.

7. Task karo paisa pao wale groups

Telegram ya WhatsApp par "like karo, review do, roz kamao" wale kaam pehle chhota payment dete hain, phir bada "investment" maangte hain. Yahi sabse zyada paisa doobne wala tareeka hai.

Fraud ho jaaye to der mat karo. Turant apne bank ko batao aur cybercrime helpline 1930 par call karo, ya cybercrime.gov.in par shikayat darj karo. Jitni jaldi shikayat, utna paisa rukne ka chance.

Thoda ruk kar sochna sabse badi security hai. Jo bhi call ya message jaldi karne ka dabav banaye, wahan se ruk jao.
