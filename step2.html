Mobile se online earning: safe tarike aur scam se kaise bache

By BomLink Team · · 5 min read

Internet par "roz ₹5000 kamao" jaise bahut se dawe milte hain. Sach ye hai ki online earning possible hai, lekin mehnat aur time lagta hai. Pehle kuch real tarike, phir scam pehchaanne ke signs.

Real tarike jo log sach mein use karte hain

  • YouTube / Shorts: gaming, tips ya tutorial content. Monetization ke liye channel ko YouTube ki requirements poori karni hoti hain.
  • Freelancing: video editing, thumbnail design, writing - Fiverr, Upwork jaise platforms par.
  • Blogging: kisi ek topic par regular likho, traffic aane par ads se income.
  • Affiliate marketing: kisi product ka link share karo, sale hone par commission.

Scam ke 5 bade signs

  • Kaam shuru karne se pehle "registration fee" maangna
  • Guaranteed bahut zyada daily income ka promise
  • OTP, UPI PIN ya bank details maangna
  • "Paise double" ya task karke turant paisa wale offers
  • Company ka koi real address ya pehchaan na hona

Golden rule: jo kaam dene ke naam par aapse paise maange, wahan ruk jao. OTP aur UPI PIN kabhi kisi ko mat batao.

Shuruat kaise kare

Ek skill chuno jo aapko pasand ho, roz 1 ghanta seekho, aur 2-3 mahine consistent raho. Shuru ki earning chhoti hogi, lekin yahi sabse pakka raasta hai.
